# AI-Powered Automatic Block Planning to Maximize Asset Availability for Train Operations on Indian Railways

**SIH 2026 | PS Code: SIH26027 | Ministry of Railways | Theme: Smart Automation**

An AI/optimization-driven system that automatically plans and coordinates maintenance
"blocks" (possessions) across Engineering, Signal & Telecom, Electrical, and Traffic
departments on Indian Railways — minimizing disruption to train operations while
ensuring all mandatory maintenance work is completed on schedule.
